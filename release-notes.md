CallCue for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit): live transcript, suggested answers and call notes for any call on your PC.

**Install**

1. Download `CallCue-Setup-1.0.0.exe` below and run it.
2. If your browser warns that the file isn't commonly downloaded, choose **Keep**.
3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The installer isn't code-signed yet.
4. In CallCue, open **Settings** and paste a free API key from https://console.groq.com/keys.
5. Under **Context**, add the role, company, job description and your resume, then join your call and press **Start call**.
